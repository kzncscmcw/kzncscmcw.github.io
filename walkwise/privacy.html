Information We Collect

We only gather data that is essential to run step tracking, health analysis and all core App features. The categories of collected data are separated below based on your device and active operations:

  1.                 Hardware & Network Device Data
    We automatically collect device model, mobile operating system version, unique advertising identifier (IDFA/GAID), public IP address, cellular/Wi-Fi network status, screen configuration, system language and time zone settings. This data helps us adapt the App to different hardware devices and resolve network connection failures.
  1.                 Service Usage & Running Log Data
    We record anonymous usage metrics including daily session duration, feature entry frequency, step tracking cycle records, page switching behaviors, crash logs and error feedback. All such statistics are aggregated to optimize tracking accuracy, fix software glitches and upgrade overall user experience without identifying individual users.
  1.                 Voluntarily Submitted Health Input Content
    WalkWise includes an AI wellness assistant module for health consultation and step goal planning. Any text you manually type into the AI chat window is collected solely to generate customized fitness advice. We never capture your photos, real-time location, medical diagnosis records, identity documents or payment information through this chat function.

How We Utilize Your Collected Information

All collected data is restricted to the following legitimate service-related purposes and will not be repurposed without your separate clear consent:

•                      Deliver full core functionality: step counting, daily step goal recording, health trend analysis and AI wellness consulting services;

•                      Optimize App running efficiency, fix system crashes, reduce lag and improve compatibility across various mobile devices;

•                      Identify abnormal login, fake step data and other improper operations to safeguard platform security and fair user experience;

•                      Receive, review and reply to your feedback, service complaints and technical troubleshooting requests;

•                      Fulfill compliance obligations set by mobile platform rules and local data protection laws;

•                      Send in-app reminders for daily step targets, health reports and service maintenance notifications.

Third-Party AI Wellness Assistant & Data Sharing Rules

Our built-in fitness consultation feature relies on an external third-party AI service to generate personalized walking and wellness recommendations. The specific data transmission rules are defined as follows:

  1.                 Transmitted Data Scope
    Only the text content you actively type into the AI wellness chat box will be transmitted to the AI service provider. We will not attach any personal identifiers, location data or sensitive health records alongside the chat text.
  1.                 Authorized Third-Party Recipient
    Your consultation text will be sent to Google Gemini AI for semantic recognition, fitness logic analysis and customized response generation.
  1.                 Strict Restrictions on Third-Party Data Usage
    The shared chat content can only be used to produce instant wellness replies for your current consultation session. The third-party AI provider is prohibited from using this data for targeted advertising, commercial marketing, user profiling or any unrelated business activities.
  1.                 User Authorization & Independent Control
    You must actively agree to the dedicated pop-up permission prompt before enabling the AI wellness chat feature. You have the right to turn off the AI assistant function anytime via the App’s Settings menu. Once disabled, the App will stop uploading any chat text to the third-party AI, and no new consultation records will be processed externally.

Scenarios Allowing Information Disclosure

We never sell, lease or trade your personal data to outside merchants for commercial marketing. Information may only be disclosed externally under the following legally compliant conditions:

•                      You have submitted clear, written or pop-up confirmed authorization for data sharing;

•                      Disclosure is mandatory to comply with national laws, administrative orders, court subpoenas or official regulatory investigations;

•                      Necessary disclosure to protect our legal rights, platform property security, or the personal safety of other App users and the general public.

Data Protection & Security Measures

We implement comprehensive technical and administrative safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, data leakage, tampering and permanent loss of your information:

•                      All data transmitted between your mobile device and our backend servers is encrypted with standard TLS transmission protocols;

•                      Chat consultation text sent to the third-party AI service is processed in real time and will not be stored on external servers for unnecessary long-term retention;

•                      Internal access to user data is limited to authorized operation and maintenance staff, who are bound by internal non-disclosure agreements;

•                      We conduct periodic security vulnerability scans to update protection mechanisms against emerging network risks.

Children’s Privacy Protection

WalkWise: Smarter Steps,Better Health is designed exclusively for users aged 13 and older. We do not actively or knowingly collect any personal data from minors under 13 years old. If we discover that personal information of underage users has been unintentionally collected, we will erase all relevant records from our server storage immediately. Parents or legal guardians who find their child has shared personal data with us may contact our support mailbox to request full data removal.

The AI content cannot replace professional medical examination, clinical diagnosis, formal treatment plans or licensed physician guidance. If you experience persistent physical discomfort or chronic illness, please seek help from qualified medical personnel promptly.
